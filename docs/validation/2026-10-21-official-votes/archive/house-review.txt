## Blocking defects

**1. The source-proof doc makes false and stale provenance claims.**
- `docs/house-metadata-compatibility-source-2026-10-21.md:47` says "No original receipt, body, failed manifest **or active Python source was changed**." The patch does change active source: `controller-repair-review.patch:105-108` (`adapter.py:312`, the chamber removed from `expected`) and `:129-130` (`bridge.py` `raw_house`). Line 3 of the same doc says the repair was "integrated locally", so the doc contradicts itself.
- `:49` says "No derived acquisition manifest was generated in this workbench." But `house-revalidated-committee-1/` now exists and feeds the replay PASS. The doc never mentions it, its hash (`6d6d4d44…`), or `revalidation.json`.
- Minor: `:51` is missing spaces ("all1,989", "HTTP200", "rejected623").

**2. The derived run artifacts pass themselves off as the original acquisition, and nothing hash-links them back.**
- `house-revalidated-committee-1/window-run.json:11,17-18` keeps the original capture's `started_at`/`finished_at` (`23:38:34.600055`) next to `status: "complete"`. At that time the capture had actually finished `incomplete` (original `window-run.json:16-18`).
- `acquisition/manifest.json:13,22` keeps `finished_at 23:38:34.529979` with `status: "acquisition_complete"` and an unchanged contract and `acquisition_semantics`. Nothing in the file marks it as derived or names the original manifest's sha256.
- The 623 rescued records become `outcome: "acquired"`, `reason: null` with no record of their original outcome (`revalidate_capture.py:29`, `:59-65`).
- The only link back is a free-text `reason` (`revalidate_capture.py:69`). `revalidation.json` points forward to the derived manifest, but nothing points back to it by hash. The replay `summary.json:230-246` `runFiles` hashes `window-run.json`, the manifest and the inventory, but not `revalidation.json`. So the replay PASS can't show from its own records that it ran on an offline-revalidated derivative rather than a real complete acquisition.
- Fix: add in-band `derived_from` fields (both original sha256s), plus `revalidated_at`, the `revalidation.json` sha256, and per-record `original_outcome`/`original_reason`, or have the replay bind `revalidation.json`.

**3. `revalidation.json` doesn't bind the logic that made the decision, and one field is a hardcoded constant.**

- `new_http_requests: 0` is also a literal, but the netns guard at `:33-34` backs it. `original_receipts_and_timestamps_preserved` is backed by the hash re-check at `:71`. Those two are acceptable.
